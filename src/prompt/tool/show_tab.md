Raise the browser tab you are working in, in front of the owner's other windows, and report where it is: the Chrome profile, the page title and the address.

Call it only when the owner must look at or act in that page — something to read, sign in to, approve or finish. Never for your own convenience: you already read the page through `mahbot chrome`, and a raise takes the focus from whatever they were doing. Raise only a page the owner must have.
