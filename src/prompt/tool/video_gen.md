Generate a clip with an AI video model, or restyle a clip you already have. The inputs choose one of three mutually exclusive shapes: a new clip from `prompt` alone (optionally one reference picture in `images`), a new clip anchored on `first_frame` and/or `last_frame`, or an edit of `video_url` guided by up to 9 `images`. `prompt` is one text input for all three.

A local path must be inside your own workspace; a frame anchor or an edit's guiding picture may also be a public HTTPS URL. An edit takes its length from its source clip.

This changes what a model renders; it does not assemble a recording. Cutting, trimming, retitling and sound over real footage belong to `hyperframes` when it is in your tool set. Each call bills a paid job and may take up to an hour — never repeat a submitted call, even a stalled or timed-out one. The result is the path to the video, marked `[VIDEO:path]`, followed by the clip's timeline of frames, speech and sound labels; name the marker in your reply to send it, and send the result before making another call.
