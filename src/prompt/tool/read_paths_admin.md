Any path on this machine is readable — your home directory, other users' folders, the product's own data directory, system paths. Relative paths resolve from the workspace; `~` and `~/…` mean your home directory, and `~user` is refused — spell another user's path absolutely instead. The one place closed to reads is the product's own live databases (`core.db`, `logs.db` and their journal tails, in the product's `db` directory): refused by where they are and never opened, because the service holds their locks. Read the product's own data with `mahbot_debug`; copies of those databases are ordinary files.
