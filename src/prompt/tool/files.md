Arrange the files in your personal workspace: `move` a file or folder into a folder of your choosing, `rename` it in place, or `delete` it for good. `operation` and `path` are required. `move` takes `to` — the destination folder, created when missing — and an optional `name`; `rename` takes `name`.

`generated/` and `uploads/` are swept: move what you want to keep into a folder of your own.

Answers with what was moved, renamed or deleted.
