Edit a real recording into a finished video file, or render an authored HTML composition the same way: footage sits on a timeline as timed segments you cut, trim and retime at a constant playback rate, lay titles and overlays over it in paint order, and carry sound on its own track. Local: no per-render cost, no timeout.

`inspect` checks a composition without rendering it; `render` writes the file you name (never overwriting one that exists) and answers with a `[VIDEO:path]` marker followed by the clip's timeline — frames, speech and sound labels. Name the marker in your reply to send it. Deliver one finished file, not a series of drafts.
