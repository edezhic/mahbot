The engine serves single-file lookups too, so an empty result from a served search means it ran and matched nothing; a search it recognises but cannot serve fails as a tool error, not as "no matches". Write `mahbot` plainly, never through a launcher or a path. A search another program owns, or a grep word glued to `{` or a redirect operator, is left to the platform and shows its own outcome. Double-quote the search's arguments: `'`, `^` and parentheses are read differently here. A word with two `%` is refused; a single `%` and every `!` are literal.
