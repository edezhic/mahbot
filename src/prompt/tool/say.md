Speak a short progress note or a very brief summary of a result out loud to the user.

The phrase is at most 100 characters, counted after trimming the surrounding whitespace; nothing is spoken for a longer one — say it in one short phrase of at most 100 characters instead, because long text is not read aloud. Nothing is spoken either while the user has speech output turned off or while nobody is at the computer. The call returns once the phrase has been rendered: a phrase the engine refused is reported as nothing spoken, with the reason.

Write the phrase in the language the user speaks. The speech plays in the background, and a newer phrase stops the one being spoken.
