Speak a short progress note or a very brief summary of a result out loud to the user.

The phrase is at most 100 characters, counted after trimming the surrounding whitespace; nothing is spoken for a longer one — say it in one short phrase of at most 100 characters instead, because long text is not read aloud. Nothing is spoken either while the user has speech output turned off.

Write the phrase in the language the user speaks, so the speech comes out in that language. The call returns as soon as the engine has the phrase; a newer phrase replaces one still being spoken.
