`action` `cancel` with `job_id` (the id from its dispatch acknowledgement) stops that quick round instead of starting one — such a call carries no dispatch argument. Its analysts stop and its result is never delivered to you, so cancel as soon as the answer is no longer needed. An id that is not one of your own dispatched quick analyses — another agent's round, a long-running investigation, a coder round, or one that names nothing — is refused and left untouched.
